Senior Backend Engineer - Stripe

About the role
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join the Payments Infrastructure team. You will design and operate the services that move billions of dollars a year.

Responsibilities:
Architecture: Design distributed systems that stay correct under partial failure.
Implementation: Write production Go and Ruby, with a bias toward simple, boring solutions.
Reliability: Own on-call for your services. Drive down p99 latency and error budgets.
Mentorship: Review code and grow engineers around you.

What we look for
7+ years building backend systems at scale.
Deep experience with Go, Ruby, or Java in production.
Strong grounding in PostgreSQL, Kafka, and distributed systems fundamentals such as idempotency and exactly-once delivery.
Comfort with Kubernetes, Terraform, and AWS.
Excellent written communication and a collaborative approach to code review.
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
